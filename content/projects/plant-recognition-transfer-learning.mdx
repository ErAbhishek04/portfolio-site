### TechStack
*Python, NASNet-Mobile, TensorFlow, Keras, OpenCV*

Fine-tuned NASNet-Mobile via transfer learning, adapting pretrained ImageNet weights to classify plant leaf category across 44 tree species[cite: 25]. 

* Built preprocessing pipelines including image normalization, augmentation, and feature extraction to improve model robustness across varied lighting and environmental conditions[cite: 26].
* Achieved 93.16% accuracy on the Malyakew dataset, outperforming baseline CNN models by leveraging NASNet's progressive neural architecture search design[cite: 27].
