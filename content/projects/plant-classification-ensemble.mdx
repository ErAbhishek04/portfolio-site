### TechStack
*Python, ResNet-50, TensorFlow, OpenCV*

Built an ensemble of CNN architectures combining ResNet-50 with complementary models to improve classification robustness across plant species[cite: 20]. 

* Trained and evaluated the model on the Leafsnap dataset comprising 184 plant species classes for fine-grained leaf identification[cite: 21].
* Achieved 97.08% classification accuracy on a multi-class plant dataset[cite: 22].
* Applied concatenation-based feature fusion across ensemble models, outperforming naive mean-operator ensembling in multi-class prediction robustness[cite: 23].
